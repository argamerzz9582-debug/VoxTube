# VoxTube v1.5.0

**Release date:** 2026-11-27
**Status:** Stable

A cleaner top bar, a search button that knows where you are, a music mini player that stays in the music section, and springier pills.

## Download

| File | What it is |
|---|---|

| `voxtube-foss.apk` | FOSS build without the built-in updater and Discord integration (also as `voxtube-foss-arm64-v8a.apk` and `voxtube-foss-armeabi-v7a.apk`) |

Android 8.0 or newer. Updates over VoxTube 1.4.0, 1.3.0, 1.2.0, 1.1.1, 1.1.0 and 1.0.0 in place.

## New

- The search button beside the tab bar searches music when you are in Music, and everything else elsewhere
- The music mini player shows only in the music section; songs keep playing everywhere else. Turn it off in Settings > Player appearance > Only in Music

## Improved

- Home and Music drop the search button from the top bar while the search button beside the tab bar is shown
- Play, Shuffle and glass pill buttons and filter chips sink with a springy bounce when pressed, with a light tick on the pill buttons
- A song you start outside Music keeps its mini player on that page, so it never seems to vanish
