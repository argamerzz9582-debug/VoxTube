VOXTUBE CHANGE LOG
VERSION: 1.2.0
DATE: 2026-11-27
STATUS: stable

NEW
- Reduce transparency setting in Appearance turns every glass bar, player and sheet solid
- On Shorts the tab bar rests folded to one glass button at the side; tap it to switch tabs
- Liked songs and videos get a small pop on the heart or thumb, with a tap of vibration
- Pull to refresh gives a tick when you have pulled far enough to refresh

IMPROVED
- Glass is easier to read over bright thumbnails
- Glass stops bending light when system animations are off, and turns solid when system contrast is raised
- The selected tab icon shows your accent colour
- Lists fade softly under the header and toward the tab bar
- Player toggles and the like and download buttons share one glass pill each, and press in under your finger
- The mini player tucks beside the shrunk tab bar, and tab icons pop when opened, by default
- The full-width bottom bar style is a solid bar
